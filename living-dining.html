Living Room & Dining Room

🔵 Blue Cloths

🔵Living room + dining room only
Shelves, piano, sofa, dining table, dining chairs
Every day: use one clean Blue cloth.
Wash the used cloth daily, so a clean set is always ready. Never use a Blue cloth in the kitchen or bathroom.
Order below: tidy first, then dust top to bottom (shelves, piano, surfaces), then the dining table and chairs, then vacuum the rug, then sweep and mop the exposed floor last.
Daily every day

Tidy the room

  1. Clear cups, remotes, papers, and any clutter from the coffee table, console, shelves, and dining table
  2. Push in the dining chairs and straighten anything left on the sofa

living room shelves

Dust, top to bottom

  1. Start high: the top shelf, then work down shelf by shelf, so dust falls onto surfaces you haven't cleaned yet, never the reverse
  2. Use the Blue cloth dry first. Wipe with the grain on wood shelves
  3. Run a hand over each shelf after. If it still feels rough or dusty, go over it again with the cloth lightly misted with water
Note: never spray cleaning product directly onto wood shelves or the TV console, it can dull or damage the surface over time. Plain water on a barely-damp cloth is enough for daily dust.

the piano

Dust the piano

  1. Close the lid and any keyboard cover before dusting the top, so dust doesn't fall onto the keys
  2. Dust the closed lid, top, and outer body with a dry microfiber cloth only
  3. If the outside still looks marked, barely mist the cloth itself with water. Never spray any liquid directly onto the piano
Never use alcohol, bleach, ammonia, furniture polish, or any spray cleaner directly on the piano keys or body, these can discolor or crack the surface permanently. Confirm with Faith whether it's acoustic or digital before doing anything beyond a dry dust, ivory, plastic, and digital keys each need different care.

Wipe the bifold glass doors

  1. Spray Magiclean Glass Cleaner onto a cloth, not directly onto the glass
  2. Wipe the lower half of each glass panel first, fingerprints and nose or paw marks collect there most
  3. Wipe the rest of each panel, top to bottom, in long, overlapping strokes
  4. Dry it right away with a second clean, dry cloth so no streaks are left
Note: don't clean the doors in direct sunlight, the cleaner dries too fast and leaves streaks behind. Because these panels are large, a rubber squeegee (wipe top to bottom, dry the blade between strokes) would make this faster than a cloth alone, worth adding to the shopping list if this becomes a daily slow point.

Check & wipe the dining chairs

  1. After every meal, brush or wipe any crumbs off the fabric seat and back of each chair
  2. Check for food marks or spills. If the seat is otherwise clean, a dry or barely-damp Blue cloth is enough
  3. If there's a fresh stain, spray Vanish Oxi Action Pre-Wash Stain Remover directly onto the mark only, not the whole seat
  4. Let it sit for the time stated on the bottle
  5. Press gently with a clean, barely-damp cloth. Never rub, rubbing pushes the stain deeper into the fabric

Vacuum the rug, slowly

  1. Vacuum slowly in straight, overlapping rows, moving the way the rug fibers lay flat
  2. Go over busy spots (in front of the sofa, the path to the dining table, where people walk most) with a second, slower pass
  3. Lift the loose threads at the rug's edge out of the way instead of running the vacuum head over them
Slow, straight passes matter more than speed here, moving too fast just pushes dirt around instead of lifting it out.

Sweep or vacuum the exposed floor around the rug

  1. Sweep or vacuum the strip of tile/wood floor around all four edges of the rug, this is where crumbs and dust collect

Mop the exposed floor: always last

  1. Sweep first, all loose dirt off the floor
  2. Fill the mop bucket about halfway with warm water (roughly 4 liters), add 1 tablespoon of dish soap, stir
  3. Dip the mop, squeeze it out well, damp not dripping
  4. Mop only the exposed tile/wood, never the rug itself
  5. Let air dry

Check the sofa covers, wash if stained

  1. Check every cushion cover and pillow cover for stains or visible dirt
  2. If everything looks clean, just fluff and straighten the cushions again, no need to wash yet
  3. If there's a stain, put Vanish Oxi Action Pre-Wash Stain Remover directly on the mark first, press gently don't rub, before it goes in the wash
  4. Unzip and remove the cover. Wash on a gentle, cold cycle with a mild detergent, no bleach, no fabric softener
  5. Air dry flat or on a hanger, never in the dryer, it will shrink the cover
  6. Put the cover back on the cushion while it's still slightly damp, it keeps its shape better than fitting it dry
Check the care label sewn into the cover before washing, and test the stain remover on a hidden corner first if this is the first wash.

Deep-dust the shelves

  1. Take everything off one shelf at a time
  2. Dust the empty shelf itself, then dust each item before putting it back
  3. Wipe glass or framed items with a barely-damp cloth
Blue clothNo product needed

Piano: wipe the outside, if marked

  1. Dust the outside first with a dry cloth
  2. If there are visible marks or fingerprints on a shiny surface, wipe with a barely-damp soft cloth in one direction only, then dry immediately with a second dry cloth. Don't let any moisture sit
  3. Don't attempt to clean the keys with any liquid. Confirm with Faith first whether this is an acoustic or digital piano, ivory, plastic, and digital keys each need different care
