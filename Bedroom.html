Bedroom

Same routine for all 3 bedrooms

🔵 Blue Cloths

🔵Dusting & surfaces
Nightstands, dresser, shelves, mirror/glass, headboard
Every day: use one clean Blue cloth.
Wash the used cloth daily, so a clean set is always ready.
Daily every day

Tidy the room

  1. Put away clothes, shoes, and any clutter from the floor, chairs, and surfaces
  2. Clear the nightstand and dresser top of anything that doesn't belong there

Make the bed

  1. Straighten the fitted sheet so it's flat and even on all sides
  2. Shake out the duvet inside its cover so it sits evenly, no bunching in one corner
  3. Lay the duvet centered on the bed, smooth flat, even overhang on both sides
  4. Fluff and arrange the pillows against the headboard

Dust, top to bottom

  1. Start at the top: shelves, top of wardrobe, headboard. Then work down to the furniture. Clean the baseboards last. Dust falls down as you clean, so top to bottom means you never have to clean the same spot twice
  2. Use the Blue cloth dry first. On wood surfaces, wipe in the same direction as the wood lines. A dry cloth picks up dust well. If you spray it wet first, the dust becomes sticky and harder to remove
  3. Run your hand over the surface after dusting. If it still feels gritty, wipe it again with the cloth barely damp with water. Wood furniture does not need cleaning spray for everyday dust
Note: never spray cleaning product directly onto wood furniture. It can dull or damage the wood over time. Plain water on a barely-damp cloth is enough for stuck-on dust.

Wipe mirror & glass

  1. Spray the glass cleaner
  2. Wipe with the Blue cloth. Use circular motions for large mirrors. Use side-to-side motions for narrow mirrors. Keep wiping until it looks clean, with no streaks

Vacuum or sweep floor

  1. Move light furniture, like chairs and bins, out of the room first, or to one side
  2. Vacuum or sweep the whole floor, including under the bed edges and inside the closet if it's open

Mop floor: always last

  1. Fill the mop bucket about halfway with warm water (roughly 4 liters)
  2. Add 1 tablespoon of dish soap (about 1 capful of the bottle) and stir to mix
  3. Dip the mop, then squeeze it out well. It should feel damp, not dripping
  4. Work in one direction toward the door. This way, you never step on the clean floor
  5. Let air dry

Empty bin

  1. Take out the bag
  2. Put in a new garbage bag

Put all products away

Put the glass cleaner and cloth back in their place. Do not leave anything out on the dresser or floor.

Weekly once a week

Change bed sheets & duvet cover

  1. Strip the fitted sheet, pillowcases, and duvet cover
  2. Turn the clean duvet cover inside out. Lay it on the bed. Place the duvet on top, matching its corners to the cover's corners
  3. Roll the duvet and cover together from the head of the bed to the foot, like rolling a sleeping bag
  4. Reach inside the open end of the roll. Grab the two far corners. Flip the whole roll over itself so it turns right-side out
  5. Unroll it. Shake it out flat. Then button or zip the cover closed
  6. Put on the clean fitted sheet and pillowcases, then make the bed as usual
Note: change the sheets every week. This is recommended by cleaning and health guidance, not just a habit. Change them sooner if needed, for example if someone is sick or a pet has been on the bed.

Dust top of wardrobe & high, unreachable areas

  1. Dust the top of the wardrobe, high shelves, and ceiling corners. These are places you do not usually see or reach

Under the bed

  1. Pull out anything stored under the bed
  2. Dust and vacuum underneath before putting items back

Clean the air purifier

  1. Wipe the outside with a cloth lightly dampened with water. Add 1 small drop of dish soap to the cloth. Keep water out of any vents or openings
  2. Take out the washable pre-filter (if it has one) and rinse or vacuum it clean
  3. Check the main filter. If it looks grey and dusty, it needs to be replaced. Check the model number for the right replacement part
Note: never use water on a HEPA filter. It will ruin the filter and it cannot be fixed. Vacuum it gently instead. General plan: wipe the outside every week. Rinse a washable pre-filter every 2 to 4 weeks. Check the main filter every 3 to 6 months and replace it every year with normal use. Check this unit's own manual for its exact filter type and schedule.
